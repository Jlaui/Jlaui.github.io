CS-499 Computer Science Capstone

Throughout the course of my time within the Computer Science program, which started back in March of 2023, I've been introduced to a variety of skills which I've had the ability to build upon. Aside from the various technical skills, I've also developed more general skills like time management and critical thinking; skills that will be utilized in any technical career I choose to pursue. The purpose of this ePortfolio is to illustrate the more technical skills that I've developed throughout the course. I focused all three of the necessary enhancement types on the same project from CS340; it involved the creation of a dashboard application for the comapany Grazioso Salvare. The dashboard is used to filer and visual shelter data in order to identify possible canidates for rescue training. This is based off of the dogs age and breed and the data can be sorted based on rescue type; disaster rescue, mountain and water. The data was not only filtered into a table but a map with pinpoints of the animal locations as well as a breed distribution pie char also are displayed.

This project displays a few different technical skills that can be applied to other pursuits in the future. For starters, the original project was created through the use of a Jupyter Notebook and a CRUD module created through Python. I was able to fully convert the project into a working Python application showing my ability to properly engineer an app with separation of concerns as well as documentation in mind throughout the process; making the code easy to understand even for a developer who didn't take part in the creation. Aside from that, in relation to the subject of algorithms and data structures, I planned and implemented an enhancement which included a ranking algorithm which evaluated and scored the animals based on multiple attributes rather than simply filtering by breeds. The improved system takes a few other factors into account, includeing the breed compatibility, age range, and chacteristics of specific animals like calm. The animals are then ranked according to these scores in order to display the overall most suitable candidates. Finally, in regards to the database aspect of this project; it previously retrieved data through the use of simple queries; I ended up implementing indexing and aggregation pipelines in order to improve the overall performance of queries. Aside from this, I also implemented query limits and pagination to improve the performance of the application when it comes to larger data sets. During this stage, I also improved the error handling with the CRUD operations. These highlight my abilities in the consideration of security and performance when it comes to my work in development.

I've learned the importance of key events in the development process including: collaborating in a team environment, communicating with stakeholders, data structures and algorithms, software engineering and database, as well as security. Although my time at SNHU was online and was limited in my communication wtih my classmates; many of my courses involved discussion posts where I was granted the chance to swap ideas and compare differing approaches to the same problem. These were all written forms of communication, and I believe it allowed me to improve my written communication, where I am typically better at expressing myself and being concise with what I'm attempting to say.

Another important element in the world of development would be communication with stateholders. This is essential in the development process as proper communication is key to understanding the requirements of what you're creating; it also allows for developers to make clear of expectations and progress to stakeholders. I gained experience through SNHU in this through the projects designed as scenarios from fictional companies. They would present a company, the problem they were having, and the requirements necessary in the software that would resolve their issue. These were also helpful in becoming familiar with real world scenarios and considering the users during creation.

As for data structures and algorithms, there were various classes that expanded my knowledge and challenged me regarding this element. The first one that comes to mind was an AI related course; we were tasked with creating an AI algorithm to find treasure in a randomly generated maze. It was by far the most challenging project in my time at SNHU and improved my critical thinking and time management skills as a whole. It introduced me to the subject of AI algorithms and the creation of them.

For software engineering and database; there are a number of projects that expanded my experience with different languages, tools and techniques for development. I have practice in creating wireframes and flow charts to document the process of designing an application as well as successfully accomplishing the planning phase. I've created applications in various languages; primarily Python, Java, C++ and have studied the software development life cycle and coding standards. I also have had experience with creating databases as well as using tools like MongoDB Compass and Postman to manage them.

Finally, for security, I was enrolled in a secure coding class that I deeply enjoyed where learned of common threats and preventative measures, received practice in testing code for vulnerabilites and fixing them, as well as studied secure coding standards. It was also impressed on me through this class, just how important security is to consider throughout the entire developmental process.

Overall, my time at SNHU has exposed me to a myriad of skills and subjects in the field of computer science. It has honed my professional skills and inspired me to explore certain subjects more. For instance, the 3D modeling class, something I hadn't once considered prior to me taking the class, is something that I could see myself experimenting with further in the future. I also have expanded on my love of coding in Python and built up a particular interest in not only the design process, but the process of interviewing the consumers and creating user stories to heavily consider throughout the development process.
